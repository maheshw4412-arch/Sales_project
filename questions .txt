1. Revenue & Profitability (Financial Performance)
The Business Ask:
 "How are our top-line sales and bottom-line profits tracking over time,
  and where are we losing money?"

What you look for:
 Total revenue ,
  net sales,
   profit margins,
    and loss-making transactions
     or discounts.

2. Product & Inventory Performance (Merchandising)
The Business Ask:
 "Which products and categories are actually driving our revenue,
 and which ones have high return rates?"

What you look for: 
Top-selling categories/subcategories,
 high-return items,
  and unit price vs. quantity trends.

3. Customer Segmentation & Behavior (Sales & Marketing)
The Business Ask:
 "Who are our most valuable customer segments, and how do their purchasing habits differ?"

What you look for:
 Corporate vs. Consumer vs. Home Office buying behavior,
  order volume, 
  and customer satisfaction scores.

4. Regional & Operational Efficiency (Logistics)
The Business Ask:
 "Are certain regions underperforming,
  and how does delivery time impact customer satisfaction?"

What you look for:
 City/regional sales breakdowns,
  shipping duration, and payment method preferences.
