Here is a structured project building plan for your **sales_data.csv** analytics project, tailored to leverage Python, SQL, Power BI, and Excel:

## 1. Data Exploration & Cleaning (Python)

* **Inspect & Profile:** Load the dataset using Pandas to check data types, missing values, and duplicate rows.
* **Data Wrangling:** Handle missing values, fix data formatting issues (e.g., dates, currency), and remove outliers.
* **Feature Engineering:** Create useful columns like total revenue (`quantity * unit_price`), profit margins, month/year extractors, and customer segment groupings.

## 2. Database Setup & Exploratory Queries (SQL)

* **Load to Database:** Import your cleaned dataset into SQLite or PostgreSQL.
* **Metric Extraction:** Write SQL queries to aggregate key business metrics:
* Total revenue and profit over time.
* Top-performing products and categories.
* Customer purchase frequency and high-value buyers.



## 3. Interactive Dashboard (Power BI)

* **Data Modeling:** Connect Power BI to your SQL database or cleaned CSV file and set up relationships if using multiple tables.
* **Visual Design:** Build an interactive dashboard featuring:
* **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Total Profit, Average Order Value (AOV), and Quantity Sold.
* **Trend Charts:** Monthly/Quarterly sales trajectory.
* **Breakdowns:** Regional performance, top 10 products, and customer segments with slicers for filtering.



## 4. Advanced Analysis & Reporting (Excel)

* **Deep Dives:** Use Pivot Tables and advanced formulas for cohort analysis, ABC analysis (Pareto principle on product sales), or basic forecasting.
* **Executive Summary:** Synthesize your findings, highlight patterns or bottlenecks, and write 3–4 actionable business recommendations.
